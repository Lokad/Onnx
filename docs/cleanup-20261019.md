## Delivered changes

Six unused members and an obsolete commented image implementation are removed.
Thirteen reference/experimental numerical kernels and their two exclusive
helpers move into [shared test support](../tests/Shared/ReferenceKernels/).
Tests and race tools retain their independent implementations and bind the
production methods separately. Three small shared production helpers are copied
unchanged into test support to keep its arithmetic independent.

The core now shares index decomposition, three fusion passes' node compaction,
binary-operation preparation and integer Resize traversal. Binary operations
carry the validated broadcast shape into the existing arithmetic loop; operator
structs select arithmetic without a per-element delegate. Representative
pooled/unpooled checks preserve output hashes and save 32–120 bytes per call.
This allocation result does not establish an end-to-end model speedup.

The nonpackable Data companion shares file-tokenizer cache/asset preparation,
preserving missing-file eviction, invalidation and per-tokenizer encode locks.
Tokenization algorithms remain in Lokad.Tokenizers. See
[source and supporting-tool maintenance](code-maintenance.md) for the new audit,
registered consumers, active standalone tools and preserved specializations.

## CPU comparison

The existing `onnx-bench` Standard_D4as_v7 VM uses an AMD EPYC 9V45 CPU.
Inference runs on CPU2, with sibling CPU3 idle and supervision on CPU0. One
shared runner per lane is staged with both frozen cores, in balanced
control/candidate/candidate/control order. Every engine/core/case has two fresh
processes and 33 retained timed calls per process.

| Workload | Frozen control, managed ms | Cleanup, managed ms | ORT ms, cleanup visits | Cleanup / ORT, both repeats |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Gemma, 128 tokens | 167.38 | 166.50 | 168.35 | 0.988, 0.990 |
| Gemma, 512 tokens | 807.31 | 799.22 | 738.48 | 1.084, 1.081 |
| e5, 30 tokens | 10.41 | 10.23 | 9.06 | 1.123, 1.136 |

The 128-token ORT-normalized change is effectively zero, with overlapping
repeat ranges. The 512-token managed median is 1.0% lower than the frozen
control; e5 is 1.8% lower. These bounded observations show no cleanup regression
in the measured cases. They do not establish a broader speedup: two process
medians are descriptive ranges, not confidence intervals. E5's native medians
vary by up to 2.8% between candidate visits, and the final control/native worker
has a raw spread warning of 31.7%. Its samples remain included. Historical
[benchmark headline figures](../BENCHMARK.md) are unchanged.

Gemma measures an owned 768-dimensional vector, including pooling, truncation,
normalization and copying. Loading, tokenization and context reset are outside
its timer. Its runner source differs from the frozen runner source only by
raising the measured compute-warmup minimum from ten to thirty seconds; the
same modified runner serves both cores, with the existing 32-call minimum.
E5 retains its existing thirty-second conditioning and isolated graph protocol.
Both use the original optimized ORT 1.29.0 library and one inference thread.

Independent terminal reload validates all 24 measured workers and three
preflight/oracle workers: 792 timed calls, complete bound files, source/core/
runner/native identities, inputs, numerical checks, affinity and process
accounting. Maximum unrelated guest CPU fraction is 0.120%. No historical 3%
A/A admission claim is made. The temporary service CPU setting is restored to
its previous inherited policy, and no owned worker remains.

The first harness attempt is preserved as rejected evidence: its ten-second
runner did not meet the wrapper's thirty-second requirement. The corrected
campaign uses a fresh directory and records the warmup-only source override.
Its evidence archive contains 371 files, verified independently on the VM and
again after transfer. Archive SHA-256:
`ad5d8b7bf5dd301b8a018f01220db33987d2683e4f29192bfe909ab361b900be`.
