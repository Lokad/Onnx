## Criteria, correctness and scope

For every shape, duplicate controls must remain within 1% in aggregate and 2% in every worker. Control-phase probe follows the same rule. Copied/actual must remain within 3% in aggregate in both phases. Both primary unpadded shapes, 30 and 128, must improve at least 3% against both copied and actual, with every probe/copied worker ratio at most 1.02. The other four shapes allow at most 2% aggregate and 5% per-worker regression. Every batch must last at least 20 ms, with no measured GC; foreign CPU at most 2% and steal at most 0.5%. These limits were not changed after observation.

All 16 timing workers pass 109,488 direct maximum cases, 1,728 complete tensors, 3,456 in-place checks and three refusals each. Maximum independent double-normalization error is `2.47770376216e-07` at the unchanged `1e-6` diagnostic limit. Inputs, masks and destination guards remain intact. Copied and candidate outputs match the actual frozen core exactly, including exceptional values and scalar/SIMD paths. Every measured input, mask and complete output hash matches the previously closed workload fixture.

Eight fresh workers per phase cover four mode rotations and their reversals. Each conditions the actual measurement routine for at least one second per shape/slot before all retained measurements. Fixed long batches and preallocated storage address the short intervals and measurement bookkeeping of the earlier experiment. Thread/process CPU clocks and all GC counts accompany wall time; no sample is removed or replaced by CPU time. Snapshot activity estimates cannot exclude hypervisor interference. Kernel controls do not establish calibrated full-model confidence.

Twenty pre-execution audit/launch-gate tests cover malformed identities, aliases, shape/order/sample coverage, clocks, outputs, every prospective criterion, all-sample arithmetic, failed/mismatched receipts and live prior processes. Collection verifies terminal process identities; the independent audit and exporter reproduce results from retained raw data.

The planned separate code capture was not launched because the candidate screen failed. The closed local proof contains optimized loop code only; final full Tier1 behavior remains unqualified. Neither the missing capture nor a revised threshold is used to promote the candidate.
