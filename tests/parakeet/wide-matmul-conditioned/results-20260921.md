# Conditioned wide MatMul probe: no candidate qualifies

The corrected comparison preserves all output bits but fails its predeclared
identical-control limits. No reduction-block or six-row candidate is promoted,
and these timings establish no kernel or application speedup.

Four fresh Windows i7-14700KF workers use CPU 2, normal .NET 10.0.12 and frozen
Core `d1f86a73`. They reuse the twelve qualified real-operand fixtures without
repeating encoder inference. All kernels, capture source and underlying fixture
bytes match the [first grid](../wide-matmul/results-20260921.md).

Each worker checks 144 distinct nonzero-accumulator/canary geometries, then every
real output after each call. The complete run retains 36,769 warmup, 20,736
conditioning and 6,912 measured calls. Twelve balanced orders cover all 132
directed predecessor pairs once per case/worker; three same-role executions
immediately precede each measurement. No samples or workers are discarded.

## Fixed controls and decision

All five combined labels at M=51 run identical public fallback code. Each cell
must have aggregate maximum/minimum <=1.03 and every worker <=1.10.

| B shape | Aggregate max/min | Largest worker max/min | Control |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `[1024,4096]` | 1.227920 | 1.893347 | Fail |
| `[4096,1024]` | 1.086389 | 1.173371 | Fail |
| `[1024,1024]` | 1.024541 | 1.047554 | Pass |

Balancing predecessors and immediate conditioning did not establish the required
stability. This does not identify a single cause for the variation; no measured
cache-miss, scheduler or hardware attribution is claimed. The earlier suspicion
of cache carryover was insufficient to explain or fix the observations.

Product-trial admission additionally required, within the declared six-cell
territory M>=64 and M%3!=0, mean cell ratio <=0.95, every cell <=1.01 and every
worker/cell <=1.05. Every candidate fails admission, including when those timing
ratios are inspected separately from the failed controls. Their apparent ratios
remain in the raw-derived analysis; none is reported as an attributable gain.
The original three-row path remains unchanged.

Numerical, coverage and resource auditing passes. There are 1,753 resource samples
and the largest process RSS is 498,507,776 bytes, below the 2 GiB limit. All workers
finish within 600 seconds, with at least 1 GiB available memory and 20 GiB free
disk. All eight worker/supervisor identities and the completion controller are
terminal. No product source, models, native tolerances or VM campaign changed.

Do not repeat this unchanged local timing experiment. The remaining reduction
and row-sharing performance question needs a different validated measurement
opportunity, such as the AMD lane after pyannote. Independent local work can
qualify the voice branch's K=4096 preparation-admission mechanism and explicit
memory budgets without claiming an unmeasured performance gain.
