Encoder position `j` has an input-time center at `320*j` samples (20 ms). The centered 400-sample Fourier window and two three-tap convolutions with strides one and two give a conservative local footprint `[320*j-520, 320*j+520)`. The regions place that footprint wholly before, across, or wholly after the original PCM end. Convolution attributes are checked against the pinned model; PCM lengths and arrays are checked against the original corpus receipt. This footprint describes the frontend and convolutions only: the clipping floor and encoder attention depend on the whole recording. It is neither a speech detector nor evidence that later frames are irrelevant.
