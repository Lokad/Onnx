# Pooled convolution: no established application speedup

All 96 public requests and resource checks pass. The fixed timing controls
fail for ORT's first crop: its two process means differ by a factor of
1.522266, above the predeclared 1.20 limit. Both managed roles pass their
repeatability checks. The observed candidate full-request mean is 1.16%
higher than the predecessor, so these data establish no speedup and do not
admit a later AMD performance candidate. Keep the failed control; no unchanged
retry is authorized by this result.
