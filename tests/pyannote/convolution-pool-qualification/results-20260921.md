The candidate reduces cumulative managed allocations by 74.8% for a complete
30-second dialogue and 75.8–75.9% for two complete ten-minute meetings. All

predecessor results. Both ordinary and exclusive speaker timelines still
match Microsoft ORT. This qualification establishes allocation and correctness
results; its historical timings are not a matched latency comparison.
