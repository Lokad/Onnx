## Selected next mechanism

The six early residual convolutions are the largest embedding nodes. Their
weights are `[32,32,3,3]`, stride/dilation one, with one-cell padding. On these
inputs, the current full patch expansion requests 91,975,680 bytes per early
convolution before pool rounding. It materializes all spatial positions before
the matrix product. A spatially tiled expansion can reduce this working set
while keeping the complete reduction dimension in its original order.

Selectively adapt the voice branch's bounded column expansion (`8af223e`) and
panel alignment (`e35653a`), then assess whether its later packing changes
(`cdeae16`, `b8849e2`, `e802581`, `5cf7dc9`) are necessary. Keep this first change
separate from activation fusion, direct/depthwise convolution, forced JIT
settings and recurrent-weight caches. Test complete outputs and all per-call
packing costs before promoting it.

This differs from the earlier default-off segmented convolution experiment:
that splits the reduction into partial sums and regressed on segmentation
convolutions. The new target is WeSpeaker's much larger dense spatial expansion;
preserving the full reduction is part of the proposed candidate contract.

ORT 1.29's [float convolution provider](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/2e2543fbe9fae542f921d47a72d21d5a4ef0b710/onnxruntime/core/providers/cpu/nn/conv.cc)
calls MLAS preparation/execution. Its [convolution implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/2e2543fbe9fae542f921d47a72d21d5a4ef0b710/onnxruntime/core/mlas/lib/convolve.cpp)
contains segmented patch expansion and GEMM traversal. This source establishes
a relevant mechanism, not which native dispatch executed in this profile.
Exact source bytes and hashes are retained separately in
`artifacts/pyannote-convolution-source-20260921/source.json`.

The subsequent recurrent opportunity remains substantial: current managed
input/recurrent projections are scalar dots per time step. ORT's
[LSTM implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/2e2543fbe9fae542f921d47a72d21d5a4ef0b710/onnxruntime/core/providers/cpu/rnn/uni_directional_lstm.cc)
hoists the input projection over the sequence and computes recurrent products
at each step. The voice branch's corresponding panels require separate
arithmetic, mutable-weight and execution-context qualification.

The current AMD application baseline remains **46.561 seconds managed versus
9.270 seconds Microsoft ORT (5.023 ratio)**. Convolution is the first candidate
to change that result, with LSTM next; Parakeet follows and Whisper is deferred.
