wider-channel, stride-two and captured-layer checks on AMD with AVX512 enabled
and disabled. It is not integrated and has no performance result yet.

sentinels, nonfinite/scalar fallbacks, dispatch and scratch accounting pass.

samples pass; peak owned RSS is 2,731,663,360 bytes. CPU2 applies to every
observed worker thread and CPU0 to the monitor. All recorded owners are
terminal; 271 collected files are verified. Preflight waits and resource
limits remain in the evidence. Worker durations are not benchmark scores.
