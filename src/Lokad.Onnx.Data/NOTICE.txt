Community-1 clustering uses the PLDA/VBx equations from pyannote.audio 4.0.0:
https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio/blob/a1ed3bb0440d33d18622e1cb6b138431cfaf4f7a/src/pyannote/audio/utils/vbx.py

VBx source is licensed under Apache License 2.0, included in Apache-2.0.txt.
Original VBx work: Lukas Burget, Pavel Palka, BUTSpeechFIT; pyannote adaptations:
Herve Bredin. The managed implementation changes storage, validation, cancellation
and orchestration while preserving the model equations. Preparation precomputes
the fixed covariance decomposition.

Clustering orchestration follows pyannote.audio clustering.py and plda.py,
Copyright (c) 2021-2025 CNRS and Copyright (c) 2025- pyannoteAI, under the MIT
license reproduced above. The managed API adds explicit invalid/no-training-data
behavior and owned results, and supports automatic speaker counts.

Community-1 windows, masks, counting and timeline reconstruction follow
pyannote.audio 4.0.0 core/inference.py, pipelines/speaker_diarization.py,
pipelines/utils/diarization.py and utils/signal.py, Copyright (c) 2016-2025 CNRS
and Copyright (c) 2025- pyannoteAI, under the MIT license reproduced above.
The managed pipeline uses deterministic canonical-label vote ties and clips
public intervals to the supplied PCM duration. Sparse masked statistics retain
the original epsilon-regularized formulas independently of the standalone
embedding API's stricter minimum-frame policy.

Whisper timestamp masking and segment/seek rules follow OpenAI Whisper:
https://github.com/openai/whisper/tree/86098128c0b4f24f0e2aa2994de830614b474227
Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI, under the MIT license reproduced above.
The managed implementation uses double log-space probability comparison,
per-window features, owned results, explicit processing limits and cancellation.
